Asked most often

How long does delivery take?

Orders placed before 2pm ship the same day and arrive in 2 to 4 days. Express reaches metro cities the next day for ₹299.

What does delivery cost?

Free on orders over ₹4,999. Below that, standard delivery is ₹149.

Which size should I order?

All sizes on the site are UK. Take your usual size in most pairs. If you are between sizes or have wide feet, go half a size up, or message us with a shoe that fits you well and we will match it.

Can I return a pair?

Yes, within 7 days of delivery, as long as the pair is unworn and in its original box. We arrange and pay for the pickup, and refund to your original payment method.

How do I know the shoes are real?

Every pair is opened and inspected by hand before it ships: stitching, materials, box label and both shoes. If a pair fails, it never reaches the shelf.
